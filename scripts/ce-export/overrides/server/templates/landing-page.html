Frequently asked questions

What is Proset?

Proset is a memory helper — an affordable, simpler AI voice notes recorder that makes sure you never lose or forget a vital idea. Record it and get a polished email, task list, or summary.

Is Proset just a voice recorder?

No — Proset goes beyond transcription. It turns recordings into actionable results: emails, tasks, and summaries.

How is Proset different from Otter, Fireflies, or other voice note apps?

Proset is simpler — one tap record, no meeting bots or setup — and more affordable, at roughly half the price of the big voice-note apps.

Which platforms does Proset support?

Proset works in any web browser and on Android.

Can I export my results?

Yes — PDF, DOCX, CSV, Markdown, and more.

Is Proset available in Spanish?

Yes — the app and documentation are available in English and Spanish (Mexican Spanish).

What happens to my recordings?

You control them — keep or delete recordings anytime.

Is Proset open source?

Yes — Proset has an open-source Community Edition on GitHub. It is the self-hostable core of the app: bring your own AI key and run it on your own server. The hosted Proset is the managed version, with cloud sync and a supported AI pipeline.
